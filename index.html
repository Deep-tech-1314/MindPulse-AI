Immediate Crisis Grounding Protocol

  1. Physical Safety: Move away from any dangerous objects and sit in a comfortable, supported chair with both feet flat on the floor.
  2. Contact Someone: Call one of the toll-free numbers above or text a trusted friend/family member: "I'm feeling unsafe and need you to stay with me."
  3. Focus on Breath: Breathe in for 4 seconds, hold gently for 4 seconds, and exhale slowly for 6 seconds.
