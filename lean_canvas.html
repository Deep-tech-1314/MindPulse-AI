LEAN CANVAS: MindPulse AI

AI-Powered Youth Mental Health Screening & First-line Supportive Companion

Aligned With: UN SDG 3 (Target 3.4 — Mental Health & Well-being)
Target: High School & University Students, Youth in Tier-2/3 Cities
Status: Functional Prototype Built & Verified • Version 1.0
1. Problem
Top 1-3 core problems faced by audience
  • Acute Stigma & Fear: Over 75% of distressed youths avoid traditional on-campus counseling due to judgment, shame, and confidentiality fears.
  • Severe Provider Deficit: Less than 1 clinical psychologist per 100,000 citizens in semi-urban areas; private therapy costs (₹1,500–₹4,000/hr) are inaccessible.
  • Delayed Intervention: Students dismiss chronic anxiety as "just exam fatigue" until symptoms escalate into acute psychological breakdowns.
Existing Alternatives

Unregulated social forums (Reddit/Discord), expensive meditation apps (Calm/Headspace - $70/yr paywalls), and crisis hotlines called only after emergencies occur.

4. Solution
Top 3 core features addressing problems
  • Zero-Stigma AI Companion ("Aura"): 24/7 empathetic conversational agent providing active listening, emotional validation, and CBT reframing.
  • Clinical Screeners: Standardized self-assessments (PHQ-9 & GAD-7) with instant risk stratification and actionable guidance.
  • Neuro-Somatic Zone: Guided 4-7-8 and Box breathing visualizer paired with 5-4-3-2-1 sensory grounding.
8. Key Metrics
Key metrics indicating health and traction
  • Engagement: Daily Active Users (DAU) and 14-day retention rate.
  • Screening Volume: Number of completed PHQ-9 and GAD-7 assessments.
  • Somatic Relief: Self-reported anxiety drop pre/post guided breathing cycles.
  • Safety Escalation: High-risk cases connected to national emergency helplines.
3. Unique Value Proposition
Single clear, compelling message that stands out
"Instant, zero-cost, stigma-free mental health first-aid in your pocket — bridging the gap between silent personal struggle and professional care."
High-Level Concept

"Duolingo meets Crisis Text Line for mental wellness and early diagnostic triage."

Privacy-First Guarantee

Zero mandatory logins, zero cloud ad-tracking; 100% on-device client processing for psychological safety.

9. Unfair Advantage
Something that cannot easily be copied or bought
  • Clinical Protocol Rigor: Seamless synthesis of validated clinical psychometric instruments (PHQ-9 & GAD-7) with immediate safety emergency protocols.
  • Zero-Friction Offline Architecture: Fully operational inside standard browsers without paid LLM API dependency or app store barriers.
5. Channels
Paths to reach target users
  • University and college student wellness cells & student unions.
  • High-school counseling portals and academic stress seminars.
  • IBM SkillsBuild student ecosystem & youth NGO networks.
  • Social media mental health awareness drives (Instagram/WhatsApp).
2. Customer Segments
Target customer profiles and users
  • Primary Users: Youth & students aged 15–25 navigating exam anxiety, loneliness, depressive thoughts, or social pressure.
  • Secondary Users: Young job seekers and early-career corporate professionals in Tier-2/3 cities facing workplace burnout.
  • Institutional Beneficiaries: Colleges and universities seeking scalable first-line wellness screening tools.
Early Adopters

Undergraduate STEM and competitive exam aspirants experiencing severe academic pressure who actively seek private digital support.

7. Cost Structure
Fixed and variable operational expenses
Fixed Costs:
  • Lightweight CDN web hosting & domain registration ($10–$25/year).
  • Clinical review board & ethics advisory honorariums.
Variable Costs:
  • Natural language processing compute scaling.
  • Campus outreach collateral & student ambassador programs.
  • Compliance and data protection audit certifications.
6. Revenue Streams
Financial sustainability and monetization models
B2B Institutional Licensing:
  • Annual campus SaaS subscription providing custom portals and anonymized student wellness index for college counseling cells.
  • Corporate employee wellness subscriptions for entry-level EAPs.
Grants & CSR Funding:
  • CSR health innovation grants (IBM SkillsBuild / public health foundations).
  • Affiliate referrals to accredited tele-therapy partner clinics.
