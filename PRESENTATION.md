### Slide 2: Problem Statement & Context
* **Slide Title:** The Youth Mental Health Crisis: Silence & Stigma
* **Key Points:**
  - 75%+ of distressed youth avoid traditional campus counseling due to fear of stigma and judgment.
  - Severe deficit of professionals: Less than 1 clinician per 100,000 citizens in semi-urban areas; private therapy costs ₹1,500–₹4,000/hr.
  - Delayed identification leads to acute psychological crises.
* **Speaker Notes:** "According to WHO and national health surveys, mental health concerns among youth are escalating, yet cost, scarcity, and stigma prevent timely intervention."

---

### Slide 3: Proposed Solution
* **Slide Title:** Introducing MindPulse AI
* **Key Points:**
  - Empathetic AI Companion ("Aura") available 24/7.
  - Globally validated clinical self-screening tools (PHQ-9 & GAD-7).
  - Neuro-somatic breathing and sensory grounding tools.
  - Built-in crisis safety guardrails routing at-risk users to 24/7 helplines.
* **Speaker Notes:** "MindPulse AI acts as a digital bridge between silent suffering and professional care, offering immediate, confidential relief."

---

### Slide 4: Core Functional Modules
* **Slide Title:** Core Application Modules
* **Key Points:**
  - **Conversational Chatbot:** Active listening and CBT reframing for academic stress, panic, and loneliness.
  - **Standardized Screeners:** Objective scoring with downloadable assessment results.
  - **Calm Zone:** Paced respiration circles and grounding exercises.
  - **Mood Tracker:** Micro-journaling with longitudinal 7-day trend analysis.
  - **Emergency SOS:** Direct click-to-call for Tele-MANAS (14416) and KIRAN (1800-599-0019).
* **Speaker Notes:** "The application combines five synergistic modules into one clean, seamless interface."

---

### Slide 5: Clinical Screening Framework
* **Slide Title:** Scientific Rigor: PHQ-9 & GAD-7 Integration
* **Key Points:**
  - **PHQ-9:** Standard 9-item assessment for depression severity (0–27).
  - **GAD-7:** Standard 7-item assessment for anxiety severity (0–21).
  - Evidence-based classification thresholds with personalized self-care recommendations.
* **Speaker Notes:** "Rather than relying on unscientific quizzes, we integrated clinically proven instruments used in hospital and academic settings worldwide."

---

### Slide 6: Neuro-Somatic Calm Zone
* **Slide Title:** Somatic Grounding & Panic De-escalation
* **Key Points:**
  - **4-7-8 & Box Breathing:** Visual pacing circles activating the parasympathetic vagus nerve.
  - **Audio Synthesis:** Real-time soothing tone guidance using the Web Audio API.
  - **5-4-3-2-1 Sensory Grounding:** Interrupts panic spirals through multimodal cognitive grounding.
* **Speaker Notes:** "When someone experiences an acute panic or anxiety attack, somatic respiration immediately brings their heart rate down within 90 seconds."

### Slide 7: Technical Architecture & Privacy Guarantees
* **Slide Title:** Architecture & Privacy-First Engineering
* **Key Points:**

* **Speaker Notes:** "Privacy is paramount in mental health. MindPulse AI is built client-first, ensuring zero personal data leaks."
